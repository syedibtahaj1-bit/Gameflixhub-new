Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

GameFlix Hub ("we", "the site") is a free games site. This page explains what information is used when you visit.

Information stored on your device

Your Pulse Coins, ledger, daily quest status, streak, theme and sound settings are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and can be cleared by clearing your browser data.

Sign-in

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID through Firebase Authentication. We use them to show your name and create your referral link. We do not receive your password.

Advertising

The site shows ads, including pop-under ads, served by third-party ad networks. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads. Please review the ad networks' own policies for details.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (pages visited, device type, approximate location). This data is aggregated.

Videos

The Reels section embeds YouTube videos and may load data from the YouTube Data API. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to those videos.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
